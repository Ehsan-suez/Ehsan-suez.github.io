<h1 class="post-title">About Me</h1>

<p>
  I use computational and statistical methods to study
  infectious disease dynamics, improve epidemic forecasting,
  and support public health decision-making. My research
  spans infectious disease modeling, outbreak analytics,
  probabilistic forecasting, and model evaluation.
</p>

<h2>Current Work</h2>

<p>
  I am currently a Steven M. Teutsch Prevention Effectiveness Fellow
  at the <strong>Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)</strong>,
  working in the Center for Forecasting and Outbreak Analytics (CFA),
  where I focus on infectious disease modeling and outbreak analytics.
</p>

<h2>Doctoral Research</h2>

<p>
  I received my PhD in Bioinformatics from the <strong>University of Georgia</strong>
  in 2026, working with
  <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qZ0DYksAAAAJ&amp;hl=en">Spencer J. Fox</a>.
  My doctoral research focused on improving infectious disease
  forecasting through baseline benchmarking, scenario-informed
  forecasting, and collaborative forecasting systems.
</p>
